The Jacksonville Jaguars could win the AFC South before Christmas. And there is almost nothing the Houston Texans can do about it.

Let that sink in, Jaguars Nation. Think back to last summer. The experts looked at this division, and who did they pick? Houston. The Jaguars were the defending champions. And they still got treated like the second-best team in their own division.

Well, look at it now. It’s week ten. Jacksonville is seven and two. Houston is three and six. The Jaguars are four full games ahead with only eight weeks left in the season. And the Texans are barely hanging on.

Welcome back to Jaguars Floor. And today, we’ve got a double dose of great news. Number one, the division race is almost over, and the math is incredible. And number two, this offense is putting up numbers this franchise has almost never seen, with a quarterback who’s now firmly in the MVP conversation. Let’s get into it.

Let’s start with good news number one. The division.

Here’s the number that should terrify Houston. Five.

Count up every Jaguars win and every Texans loss over the next four weeks. If that number hits five, the AFC South belongs to Jacksonville. Before these two teams even meet again on December twentieth.

Let’s walk through it, because the math is beautiful.

If the Jaguars go three and one, and the Texans go two and two? Done. Division clinched.

If the Jaguars go two and two, and the Texans go one and three? Done.

If the Jaguars somehow lose three of their next four, but Houston loses all four? Still done.

And even if Houston wins three of its next four games, all Jacksonville has to do is win out, and the race is over.

Every single road ends in the same place. And the prize at the end of that road is a home playoff game.

Now, nobody in that Jaguars locker room is talking about the number one seed yet. And they shouldn’t be. The Bills, the Bengals, the AFC West, all of that can wait. Jacksonville has to close out its own division first. That’s job one. But if the Jaguars take care of business, that big week fifteen showdown in Houston turns into just another Sunday. No pressure. No do-or-die. Just a formality.

So how did this race flip so fast? Because Houston keeps finding ways to lose games it should win.

Last weekend, the Texans had this one in the bag. They jumped out to a seventeen to nothing lead on the Cleveland Browns in the first half. It looked easy. It looked like Houston was finally getting its season back on track.

And then it all fell apart. The Browns came back, one score at a time. And with fifty-three seconds left on the clock, Cleveland ran it in for the winning touchdown. Final score, Browns twenty-seven, Texans twenty-four.

That wasn’t a close loss to a great team. That was a team giving a game away. And the whole AFC South felt it.

And it wasn’t the first time. Earlier this season, Houston dropped a game to the New York Giants, and that one hurt too. Add the Browns collapse, and the Texans have handed Jacksonville two gifts they never should have given.

And now look at the numbers. The Athletic’s playoff simulator says Houston has just a two percent chance to win the division. Two percent.

For Houston to pull it off, the Texans would have to win every single game left on their schedule. And they’d also need the Jaguars to go three and five the rest of the way. That’s a lot to ask from a team that can’t hold a seventeen-point lead.

And Houston’s schedule isn’t doing it any favors. The Texans still have to face the Steelers. They still have to face the Jaguars. And they still have to face the Green Bay Packers. Those are not easy games.

Now, some people around the league will say, hey, it’s the NFL, anything can happen. And that’s true. Weird things happen every week. But right now, the Texans are on life support. And the Jaguars know it.

And that leaves Jacksonville with something bigger to think about than just Houston. Because there’s a piece of history on the line here.

The Jacksonville Jaguars have never won this division two years in a row. Not once.

Here’s the part Jaguars fans have been afraid to say out loud. This franchise has never repeated as AFC South champions. Not one time.

They won the division in 2017. That team went all the way to the AFC Championship game. And then the next year? It crashed. They won it again in 2022. And the next year? The same thing happened. Every time Jacksonville gets to the top of the mountain, it seems to trip on the way back down.

So the big question coming into this season was simple. Was last year’s division title just another one-hit wonder?

Nine games in, that question has an answer. And it’s a loud one.

The Jaguars aren’t just hanging around. They’re one of the best teams in the entire AFC. And in the Liam Coen era, the only team that’s really pushed them in this division is Houston. That’s why Jaguars fans check the Texans score every single week. Right now, every Houston loss feels like a Jaguars win. And they’ve been getting plenty of those lately.

But nobody inside that building is calling it done. And there’s a good reason for that.

Some of these players were on the team back in 2023. That year, Jacksonville started eight and three. Eight and three! It looked like a sure thing. And then the season fell apart, and the Jaguars missed out on the division. These players remember how fast a sure thing can disappear.

So the Jaguars still have work to do. They still have to win their last three division games. And one of them is in Houston. But if they do it? They don’t just make franchise history with the first-ever back-to-back AFC South titles. They also put themselves in line for a top seed in the AFC.

First, though, they have to get through the next four weeks. So what does the road look like?

Next up is a trip to face the New York Giants. And if Jacksonville wins that one, it’ll be three straight road wins for the first time since 2005. 2005, Jaguars Nation! That tells you how rare this kind of success has been.

After that, the Jaguars still have to see the Titans again and the Colts. And they’ve also got the Steelers, the Cowboys, and the Commanders on the schedule. Most of those are games a good team should win.

And that’s the point. There’s no such thing as an easy game in this league. You can never relax. But this schedule gives Jacksonville a real shot to keep stacking wins.

And the fans are feeling it. One longtime fan wrote in to the team’s beat writer and said he hasn’t felt this confident about the Jaguars since he was in sixth grade, back in 1999. 1999! That was the team that went fourteen and two.

Another fan looked at the standings and asked, so this is how the other half lives? And the beat writer’s answer? Pretty much, yes.

For years, Jaguars fans watched other teams run this division. Now, they’re the ones doing it. And they’re enjoying every single minute.

Some of them are enjoying it a little too much. One fan even joked that defensive coordinator Anthony Campanile is going to become a head coach somewhere next year. And the beat writer’s reply was short and fair. Take it down a peg or two.

Good advice. Because nothing is won yet. But there’s a reason this whole city is talking big right now. And it starts with the offense.

That brings us to good news number two. The Jaguars aren’t just winning. They’re putting up points like this franchise has almost never seen.

Jacksonville has now scored thirty or more points in four straight games. That ties a franchise record from 2017. One more thirty-point game against the Giants, and the record belongs to this team alone.

And here’s what makes it wild. The last time the Jaguars did this, back in 2017, they made it all the way to the AFC title game.

And think about where this franchise has come from. This is the same franchise that once went fifty-eight straight games without scoring thirty points. Fifty-eight! And this team has done it six times in nine games.

Sunday’s forty to twenty-three win over the Titans was the perfect example. The Jaguars never trailed. Not once. They won by seventeen points. And according to one fan, they scored on eight of their ten possessions. Eight of ten!

But here’s what I love. Head coach Liam Coen wasn’t fully happy. He talked about meat left on the bone. And he had a point. Several first-half drives stalled out before reaching the end zone.

Kicker Cam Little made four field goals, from forty-six, forty-eight, thirty-six, and forty yards. That’s a great day for a kicker. But it also means the Jaguars settled for three points in spots where they could have had seven.

Still, Little deserves a ton of credit. That was his second week in a row with four field goals. And he’s now made fourteen straight this season. Fourteen in a row. When your kicker is that automatic, every drive that gets past midfield turns into points.

And when you can score on eight of ten possessions and still feel like you left points out there? That’s a scary thought for the rest of the AFC.

The response to that fan who pointed out the eight-of-ten stat was simple. These are the good days.

And that’s the real story here. At seven and two, riding a three-game winning streak, the Jaguars might not have even hit their best level yet.

And the man who makes all of it go is playing so well that people are starting to put his name next to three big letters. M. V. P.

Trevor Lawrence is playing the best football of his career. And the numbers back it up.

Against the Titans, he went twenty-five of thirty-five for three hundred fifty-two yards and three touchdowns. That’s his biggest passing day of the season. And the most he’s thrown for in two years under Liam Coen.

And it’s not just one game. Over the last four games, Lawrence has thrown for one thousand one hundred ten yards, eleven touchdowns, and only two interceptions. Eleven touchdowns to two picks over a month of football. Those aren’t just good numbers. Those are the numbers of a quarterback who belongs in the MVP conversation.

But it’s not just what he’s doing. It’s how he’s doing it.

A fan wrote in asking about Lawrence’s decision-making. And he nailed it. The old Trevor throws, the ones that looked bad the second they left his hand? They’re gone.

Even the one interception against the Titans bounced off a tipped ball. And this season, most of his picks have been flukes. A tipped pass. A drop. Or a deep shot that basically worked like a punt. That’s a completely different quarterback than the one who used to force throws into coverage.

And that matters. Because the flashy plays get you on the highlight shows. But the smart, boring plays win football games. And right now, Trevor Lawrence is doing both.

He also just made history. Lawrence now has twenty thousand sixteen career passing yards. And he became just the fourth quarterback ever to throw for twenty thousand yards, throw one hundred fifteen touchdown passes, and run for twenty touchdowns in his first six seasons.

The other three? Josh Allen. Cam Newton. And Dak Prescott.

That’s elite company. And Lawrence says it all comes down to reps. The more this offense runs these plays together, the more comfortable everybody gets.

But he’s not doing it alone. Liam Coen said it best. You never know which player will step up on any given Sunday. And last week, it was a receiver who had the game of his life.

Parker Washington.

Washington just had the best day of his career. And he did most of it before halftime. He caught six passes for one hundred forty-eight yards in the first two quarters alone. And he finished the game with seven catches for one hundred fifty-two yards and a touchdown.

And every one of his big plays led to points. He caught a two-yard touchdown on the opening drive. After that, even when drives stalled, he kept the points coming. A twenty-yard catch along the sideline set up the first field goal. A fifty-yard catch-and-run set up the second. And a thirty-two-yard grab on the final drive of the half set up the third.

And almost all of it came out of the slot, where he can cut either way and beat you short, deep, or along the sideline.

And the numbers say this is no fluke. Parker Washington leads the entire NFL at seventeen point nine yards per catch. And he’s gotten better every single year. In 2023, he averaged eight point three yards per catch. Then twelve point two. Then fourteen point six. And now seventeen point nine.

That’s a player who’s grown every season. The Jaguars have turned him into a real number one receiver. And his next contract is getting more expensive every week.

One fan wrote in saying he didn’t see Washington much in the second half. But that’s not true, he played the entire second half. Another fan summed it all up in three words. Pay Parker.

And he’s not the only one eating.

Travis Hunter keeps making big plays. He came up with an interception against the Titans off pressure on the quarterback. And people are already starting to talk about an All-Pro case for him. Think about that. A two-way star making game-changing plays and building an All-Pro résumé.

And then there’s sixth-round rookie Josh Cameron. He’s got three touchdown catches in nine games. He scored in the red zone against the Browns back in week one, and he’s kept producing ever since. A sixth-round pick making plays in the red zone. That’s the kind of depth that separates good teams from great ones.

So let’s put it all together, Jaguars Nation. Your double dose of great news.

Good news number one. The AFC South is almost locked up. Jacksonville is seven and two. Houston is three and six. The Jaguars are four games up with eight to play. If Jaguars wins plus Texans losses add up to five over the next four weeks, the division is clinched before the two teams even meet on December twentieth. Houston blew a seventeen-point lead to Cleveland. The Athletic gives the Texans a two percent chance. And this Jaguars team is on the verge of doing something no Jaguars team has ever done. Win back-to-back division titles.

And good news number two. This offense is on fire. Four straight games with thirty-plus points, tying a franchise record. Six thirty-point games in nine weeks, from a franchise that once went fifty-eight straight without one. Trevor Lawrence with eleven touchdowns and two picks over his last four games, joining Josh Allen, Cam Newton, and Dak Prescott in the history books. Parker Washington leading the NFL in yards per catch. Travis Hunter building an All-Pro case. Cam Little making fourteen straight field goals. And a sixth-round rookie scoring touchdowns.

These are the good days, Jaguars Nation. Enjoy them. But don’t take them for granted. Because the job isn’t done yet. The Giants are next. One more thirty-point game sets the franchise record. And one more win keeps this team marching toward history.
